Senate Movement for Olofu 2027 is restricted to Benue South's nine LGAs. It uses a separate database, session cookie and SENATE_MOVEMENT_* configuration. No accounts, credentials, personal photos or operational database were copied. Photos and administrator credentials are reserved for the owner.

The location directory is an exact Benue South subset of NOPREM's supplied Nigeria_polling_units.csv snapshot: 1,373 PUs in 102 wards. Source hashes, per-LGA counts and upstream commit are retained in data/benue_directory_source.json. Current official completeness has not been independently verified. The separate 2023 historical baseline covers 236 Apa/Agatu PUs only; other voter totals remain unknown.
